What it does

A multiviewer that stays out of the way.

Pick a layout, pick the cameras.

Nine layouts from one big picture to a 4×4 grid. Click a tile to give it a camera, double-click to see it full size, Esc to go back.

  • Finds NDI sources on its own
  • Audio meters on every camera, and listen to any one
  • Light preview streams for small tiles, full quality when big
  • Names, clock and full screen
  • Remembers everything for next time

Steer PTZ cameras from the tile.

A joystick for pan and tilt, zoom and focus, a speed slider for slow on-air moves, and eight presets. Works with PTZ cameras that take control over NDI.

  • Hold to move, let go to stop
  • Click a preset to go there, hold to save it
  • Keyboard control too

Streams next to your cameras.

Paste an SRT, RTMP, RTSP or HLS address, or a YouTube link, and it plays in a tile. An encoder or an ATEM can send straight to it.

  • SRT and RTMP, pulled or pushed to you
  • YouTube videos and channel live links
  • Reconnects on its own
Showcaller Studio license

Switch your ATEM from the multiviewer.

Type in your ATEM's IP address and a switcher bar appears under the tiles. Link a tile to an ATEM input and it lights red on program and green on preview. Unlocks with a Showcaller Studio license key; the rest of the multiviewer stays free.

  • Program and preview rows, cut, auto and fade to black
  • Mix, dip or wipe, plus record and ISO on the SDI Extreme ISO
  • Click a tile to preview it, Space to cut, Return for auto

Watch from anywhere

Built-in Tailscale status and invite codes, so a client can watch your cameras from home without Remote Desktop.

Light on the network

Small tiles use NDI's preview stream, so a full grid over a VPN stays smooth.

Made for sets

From the people behind Showcaller Studio, the playback system for screens on sets and stages.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes, for any use. The one paid part is ATEM switcher control, which unlocks with a Showcaller Studio license key. If you need screens on a set, LED walls or projection run from one place, have a look at Showcaller Studio.

Which cameras work?

Anything that sends NDI, including NDI PTZ cameras from BirdDog, PTZOptics, Panasonic, Sony and others, plus NDI from software like OBS, vMix and Showcaller Studio. Camera control needs a PTZ camera that accepts it over NDI.

Which ATEM switchers can it control?

ATEM switchers that take control over the network, such as the ATEM Mini range, Television Studio and Constellation models, and the SDI Extreme ISO. Record and ISO buttons appear on models that record. Put the multiviewer on the same network as the switcher and type in its IP address.

Does it play sound?

Yes. Every NDI camera with sound shows a level meter on its tile, and you can listen to one tile at a time through your speakers. YouTube tiles have their own sound button. SRT, RTMP and other streams are picture only.

Can a client watch from home?

Yes, if your cameras' network is on Tailscale. Make an invite code in the app's settings and send it to them. They paste it in and the cameras appear.
