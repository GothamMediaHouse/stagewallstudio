A full production switcher for NDI shows.

Showcaller Switcher is a two M/E switcher that runs on a Mac. Cut and mix NDI cameras, key graphics and lower thirds, play clips, mix the sound and record the show, all from one control panel, with a multiview on a second screen.

What it does

Everything a small control room needs.

A panel that looks like a real switcher.

Laid out like a hardware panel, with coloured rows for the aux, M/E 2, M/E 1 and the downstream keys, a name on every button and tally that shows what's on air. Press preview, then CUT or AUTO, or pull the fader.

  • Two M/Es, with M/E 2 as a source on M/E 1
  • Mix, dip and seven wipe patterns, with your own rates
  • Fade to black
  • Four aux buses, each sent out as its own NDI feed
  • 8 inputs on screen at a time, up to 20 with SHIFT

Keys, lower thirds and animated graphics.

Each M/E has a key for picture in picture, luma or chroma (green screen). Two downstream keys take a lower third, a logo, any input, or an animation with transparency.

  • Lower thirds with your text, fonts, colours and a fade, slide, wipe or rise in
  • Animated WebM and GIF graphics; a MOV with alpha is converted for you
  • Masks, levels and TIE, ON AIR and AUTO on every key

Two media players.

MP1 and MP2 are on every bus, so a clip, a sting or a holding slide is one button away. Load a pool of clips and stills, click one to cue it, and roll it from the panel.

  • Elapsed and remaining time, loop and auto play
  • Any input can also be a colour, a picture or a looping video
  • Cameras and capture cards plugged into the Mac work as inputs too

An audio mixer built in.

Every input and both media players get a channel strip. Sound follows the picture when you want it to, and program sound goes out with the NDI program feed and into the recording.

  • AFV, ON and OFF per channel, with gain and faders
  • EQ, gate, compressor and delay on each strip
  • Program limiter and EBU R128 loudness meter
  • Listen to any channel, and a master that can follow fade to black

A multiview on the second screen.

Preview and program up top, every input below with names, tally and audio meters, plus the media players, M/E 2, the aux buses and a clock. Eight layouts to choose from.

  • Red and green tally on every box
  • Choose what goes in each box
  • Labels, meters and safe areas on or off

Record the show

One button records program or clean feed to a .mov in ProRes 422, ProRes LT or H.264, with sound and timecode.

NDI outputs

Program, clean feed and four aux outputs in 720p, 1080p or 4K at 25 to 60 fps, ready for a stream encoder, a recorder or another app.

PTZ cameras

A Cameras page with pan, tilt, zoom, focus and presets for PTZ cameras that take control over NDI.

Practice cameras

It starts with eight practice cameras, so you can learn the panel before any NDI gear is plugged in.

Questions

Good to know.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon or Intel, and a second display if you want the multiview full screen (with one display it opens in a window). NDI cameras are optional; it comes with practice cameras. A Windows version is on the way.

Does it take SDI or HDMI?

Yes, through a capture card or a USB capture device the Mac can see. Outputs are NDI; to get SDI or HDMI out, send the NDI program to a converter or to Showcaller Screens.

Can I stream with it?

Send the NDI program feed, with sound, to your streaming software or encoder, such as OBS or vMix. The switcher records the show itself as well.

Which hardware panels work?

Elgato Stream Deck, X-keys and MIDI controllers, plus anything that can send commands through Bitfocus Companion. Our own Showcaller Panel, with lit keys and a T-bar, is coming soon. The panel support is new, so tell us what you have and we'll help set it up.

Is my Showcaller Screens license enough?

No. A Screens key covers Screens and the Pro features in Timer and Multiview. The Switcher has its own key, like Replay, ISO and Playback.

Pricing will be set when it's out of testing. Testers get the 14-day trial and can ask us about longer test keys.
