FFmpeg

Showcaller Hub, Link, Multiview, ISO, Replay, Playback, Stream and Switcher include FFmpeg, which they run as a separate program to record, convert and stream video. These builds have no GPL or “nonfree” parts. The apps don’t change FFmpeg, and you may replace the app’s resources/ffmpeg with your own build.
