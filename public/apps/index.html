Screens comes with Pro

A Showcaller Screens license also unlocks the Pro features in Timer and Multiview. Replay, ISO, Playback and Switcher are licensed on their own.

Updates in one click

When a new version is out, the app says so. Click Update, or Update all in the Hub. Updates are signed, so a computer only installs the real thing.

Keys in one place

Add your keys to the Hub once. Every app on that computer picks them up straight away, even the ones already open. Every paid app runs free for 14 days first.
