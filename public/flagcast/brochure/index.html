What your players get

Three things that change the day

Par 3s, live

A camera on every par 3 tee and green. Each shot plays live on screens around the club and in the app, so nobody misses the ace.

  • Instant replay of every tee shot
  • Closest-to-the-pin measured and posted on the spot
  • Hole-in-one moments saved for the dinner

A live leaderboard

Scores go in on the course and the board updates in seconds, by flight, net and gross. No more waiting on a whiteboard at the turn.

  • Big screens in the clubhouse, tent and halfway house
  • The same board on every phone
  • Results ready the moment the last group finishes

A show for the dinner

We cut the day into a highlight reel while you play. It runs at awards, along with winners, sponsors and your fundraising total.

  • 2 to 3 minute highlight reel
  • Winner graphics for every flight and contest
  • Clips sent to players to share the next day

The app

Scoring that players actually use

Each cart scans a QR code at check-in. No downloads, no passwords. One player keeps score for the group, and the leaderboard does the math, handicaps included.

Any format

Stroke play, best ball, scramble, shamble, Stableford and match play, with full or percentage handicaps.

Flights and nines

Shotgun or tee-time starts, multiple flights, front and back nine leaders, member-guest match brackets.

Watch the par 3s

Every par 3 cam lives in the app. Players see the group ahead tee off and catch replays at the turn.

Contests built in

Closest to the pin, long drive, hole-in-one prizes and skins, posted to the screens as they happen.

For the committee

A tournament desk to fix a score, settle a tie with a card-off, and lock final results.

Keeps the field moving

See which groups are on which hole, and spot a slow group before it backs up the course.

Run of show

How the day runs

6:00 AMWe set up

Cameras go out to the par 3s, screens go up in the clubhouse. Nothing for your staff to do.

8:00 AMCheck-in

Each cart gets its QR card. The screens show pairings, start holes and a welcome from your sponsors.

9:00 AMShotgun start

Scores flow in. Par 3 shots play live and in replay, contest leaders post as they change.

1:30 PMLast putt

Final results lock as soon as the committee signs off. No hand tallying.

3:00 PMAwards

Highlight reel, winners for every flight and contest, the fundraising total and a thank-you to every sponsor.
