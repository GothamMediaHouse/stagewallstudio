Setup

Format

Handicaps on the Teams tab are each player’s course handicap. The allowance is applied to it, and strokes fall on the holes by stroke index. A picked-up ball counts as net double bogey. Ties are broken on the back 9, 6, 3 and last hole.

Course

Stroke index (SI) 1 is the hardest hole. Use the numbers on the club’s scorecard.

Teams

Each team gets its own scorecard code and QR card. Start hole is for a shotgun start. For a scramble, set the team handicap.

One team per line, columns split by tabs or commas: Team name, Flight, Start hole, Player 1, Handicap 1, Player 2, Handicap 2, and so on (up to 4 players). Copy the rows straight out of Excel or Google Sheets.

Scores

Fix any score here. Changes save as you leave each box and show on every screen. Type 0 for a picked-up ball, clear the box to remove a score.

Contests

Closest to the pin is measured in feet (4.5 = 4 ft 6 in). Long drive in yards. Results go on the screens as soon as you add them.

Set the contest’s hole and name, then Save before entering results.

Screens and sponsors

Announcement

Par 3 cameras

For each par 3, paste the camera’s https link: an HLS stream (.m3u8, for example from Showcaller Stream or a cellular bonding unit) or a web player link such as a VDO.Ninja view link. Leave it empty to show a drawing of the hole.

Sponsors

Pick where each sponsor shows. Hole, camera and contest spots use the hole numbers. The link and offer show on the players’ phones, and taps are counted for the report. Logos are small pictures (PNG or JPG, under 140 KB).

Staff and playlists

Golf pros and marshals

They sign in to the Sunday Pin app with the tournament code and their PIN. A golf pro can fix scores, switch screens, take over the screens and nudge a cart. A marshal can only post contest results.

Screens at the club

Add each TV or LED wall and pick what it plays, in order. Open its link full screen on that TV. A screen with an empty list shows everything.

Schedule

Shows on the Schedule page of the screens.
